What we do

Three specialties, done properly by the same crew from prep to final coat.

Accent walls

Custom geometric wall designs in matte black, deep greys and soft sage, with optional gold detail lines. Built to fit your room, around outlets, vents and sloped ceilings.

Painting

Interior painting with careful cutting-in, patched surfaces and a smooth, even finish that holds up to daily life.

Epoxy floors

Flake-finish epoxy for garages, sheds and stairs. We grind and prep the concrete first so the coating bonds, then seal it for a tough, easy-to-clean floor.
